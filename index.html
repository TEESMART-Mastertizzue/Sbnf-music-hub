ABOUT SBNF MUSIC HUB

SBNF means Soul Beyond Normal Frequency. We believe every artist deserves an opportunity to grow, express creativity and reach a wider audience. Our mission is to build opportunities for independent music creators in Africa and beyond.

WHAT WE OFFER

Music Distribution

Support and guidance for delivering music to digital streaming platforms through approved distribution partners.

Artist Development

Helping independent artists improve their identity, music presentation and career direction.

Music Promotion

Promotional support to help artists introduce their music and connect with listeners.
